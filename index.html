Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Need a tailored arrangement? Our team is ready to discuss your specific requirements.

How far ahead should I request a booking?

We recommend requesting your vehicle as early as possible, particularly for events, group movements, or high-demand periods.

Can ANTRAC arrange airport transfers?

Yes. Share your arrival or departure details in your booking request and we will help coordinate the appropriate transfer.

Are prices shown final?

Published rates are daily rental rates. ANTRAC confirms availability and any itinerary-specific requirements before accepting a booking.

Can you support group and event transportation?

Yes. ANTRAC provides bus, corporate, and event transportation arrangements for organised movements.
